# Source documents guide

Internal reference material for verifying WPI Whisper website content. These files support research and drafting. They are **not** public website assets and must not be copied wholesale into pages.

Before drafting or updating team-specific copy in any milestone, inspect every relevant document in this folder and evaluate each fact for currency, privacy, and public appropriateness. Presence in the repository is not permission to publish.

## Documents

| Document | Primary authority | Best used for | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Ultimate Frisbee Constitution.docx` | Strongest source for formal, relatively stable governance | Club purpose, membership/eligibility, competitive vs league structure, executive roles, elections, coaches, non-discrimination | Revised 2023; confirm that procedural details still match current practice before publishing season-specific claims |
| `Frisbee FAQ (for club fair).docx` | Strong source for prospective-player messaging | What ultimate is, experience expectations, fall league vs competitive team, general practice cadence, PE credit | Intake channels (forms, Slack links) may be stale; do not publish contact methods without confirming they are current and approved |
| `THE Transition Doc.docx` | Operational / institutional knowledge for officers | Culture themes, seasonal workflow, responsibilities, practice/tournament patterns | Contains internal procedures, personal names, vendor contacts, informal language, and sensitive social-event detail. Validate freshness. Prefer Constitution for governance conflicts |
| `Bullets Spirit Manual.docx` | Weak / incomplete for public culture claims | Filename suggests Spirit-of-the-Game guidance, but the file currently contains sideline chants rather than conduct principles | Title uses “Bullets,” which conflicts with the public “Whisper” name and may be outdated. Do not publish chant content. Do not invent Spirit Manual rules that are not in the file |

## Standing publication rules

1. Inspect all relevant documents before drafting team-specific copy.
2. Prefer the Constitution for formal governance and membership rules.
3. Prefer the most clearly current source for changing operational details (schedules, dues, contacts, tryout timing).
4. Distinguish stable facts from season-specific information.
5. Do not publish a disputed, ambiguous, or stale fact.
6. Do not publish sensitive or internal-only material merely because it exists here.

## Do not publish from these files

- Personal phone numbers or personal email addresses unless clearly approved as public contacts
- Passwords, credentials, account access instructions, or drive/permission handoff notes
- Student IDs or private roster eligibility paperwork details
- Vendor contacts used only for officer purchasing workflows
- Internal disputes, disciplinary notes, or private officer evaluations
- Sensitive social-event / initiation details
- Private financial amounts or reimbursement workflows
- Anything clearly intended only for officer transition

## Handling conflicts and stale information

When documents disagree:

1. Prefer the Constitution for formal governance.
2. Prefer the clearest current source for operational details.
3. Leave the fact unpublished if it remains ambiguous.
4. Record the unresolved conflict in [CONTENT_INVENTORY.md](../CONTENT_INVENTORY.md) or the relevant pull-request notes.

Examples of known caution areas:

- **Team name:** Constitution and site branding use Whisper / WPI Ultimate Frisbee Club; one spirit-manual filename uses “Bullets.” Do not claim a former-name history without team confirmation.
- **Spirit Manual content:** The file does not currently provide Spirit-of-the-Game principles; public Spirit language should rely on the sport’s self-refereed nature (FAQ) and constitution purpose, not invented manual text.
- **Recruitment intake:** FAQ mentions Slack and forms without durable public URLs in the repository. Keep Join/Contact intake deferred until a monitored channel is confirmed.
- **Named people:** Transition-doc names of past officers, coaches, and staff contacts are not automatically approved for the public site.

## For future milestone agents

- Read this guide and the relevant `.docx` files before implementing Roster, Schedule, Join, Media, or Contact content.
- Extract facts first; rewrite in visitor-facing language second.
- Keep internal editorial status language out of public pages.
- Do not modify the original `.docx` files as part of ordinary website work.
